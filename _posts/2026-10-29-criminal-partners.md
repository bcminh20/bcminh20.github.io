Hi, this is my first writeup for an OSINT challenge and my first ever blog in my cybersecurity journey. I hope what I am sharing will be useful for you all. Let's get started!

- **Challenge:** [Criminal Partners - OSINT Industries CTF](https://ctf.osint.industries/challenges#Criminal%20Partners-63)
- **Personal difficulty:** 3/5
- **Personal enjoyment:** 3/5

## Scenario

Tania Gomez and Armin Moini are Swedish fugitives linked to a major organized crime investigation involving alleged drug trafficking, money laundering, and weapons offenses. Gomez was eventually arrested in Spain after years on the run, while Moini remains wanted by Swedish authorities and is believed to have international connections.

Before they became fugitives, Tania Gomez and Armin Moini were seen enjoying a date together at a restaurant on September 8, 2014.

Find the photograph, trace the location, and identify the restaurant where the couple was seen.

## Challenge Objective

Starting with the scenario and clues provided, locate the photograph of Tania Gomez and Armin Moini from September 8, 2014.

Once you find the picture, examine the available clues and identify the restaurant where the couple was seen together.

Submit the restaurant name in this format:

`OSINT{Restaurant_Name}`

## Solution

The solution follows two steps:

1. Find the correct photograph.
2. Identify the location.

### 1. Finding the Photograph

The first thing that came to my mind was that I have to find out about each individual's social media accounts, hoping to find the photo I need. There are several candidates, such as Facebook, Instagram, and X. However, we may not easily find the correct account just by entering their name and waiting for luck (I have tried, and miracle was never by my side). That's why we have to find any related information relating to each of them (relatives, companies, etc.).
